EmpathyLens PROTOTYPE

Explore accessibility beyond visual design

Guided experience · 7 steps

WELCOME

A website can look accessible and still leave people behind.

EmpathyLens is an interactive awareness prototype that helps developers understand how blind users may experience websites through screen readers.

“I can see the page, but can I understand it without seeing it?”

Meet Yasmin: Imagine applying for a job using a screen reader. If buttons have no meaningful names or form fields have no labels, completing a simple task can become confusing.

What you'll explore

Preview a sample interface, read a scripted screen-reader-style transcript, and compare examples of unclear and improved controls.

Why it matters

Inclusive design considers how people navigate, understand and interact with a page—not only how it looks.
